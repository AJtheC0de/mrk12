The problem

You highlight a line because it matters. Two weeks later the idea is gone. Not because it wasn’t important, but because nothing brought you back to it.

  1. 01

    Highlights stay in the book

    Your best ideas stay on paper, where you rarely look again.

  2. 02

    Notes take too long

    Typing out and explaining passages interrupts your reading, so it keeps getting postponed.

  3. 03

    Nothing brings you back

    Without a way to review, even strong ideas blur within days.

Second Reading Chapter 4

Most of what we read passes through us like weather. We agree with it, we underline it, and we move on to the next page, certain that something so clear will stay with us.

A book you only read once is a conversation you never finish.

The second visit is where the ideas start to answer back. What seemed obvious turns out to be subtle, and what seemed minor turns out to be the point.
